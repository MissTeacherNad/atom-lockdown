How to play

  1. Divide the class into four teams and choose a round.
  2. The highlighted team answers the question orally.
  3. Tap Reveal answer, then Correct or Incorrect.
  4. The next team is selected automatically. Three correct answers in a row earn one bonus point.
  5. At the end, the team with the highest score wins.

Triple Challenge: students receive one piece of information and must give the other two. The family round asks for the English family name.
